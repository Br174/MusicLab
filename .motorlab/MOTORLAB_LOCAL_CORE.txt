Rules:
- preferred format: <emoji> <canonical label> — <context>;
- do not emit a canonical MotorLab status emoji without its label;
- repeated-fireworks/repeated-lightning counts are obsolete as user-facing level codes;
- Watchdog may retain internal levels but visible recovery uses the canonical labeled symbols;
- 🎆 marks an action/phase/milestone completed, not necessarily the whole request;
- 💯 is reserved only for true end-to-end completion after all requested verification/delivery is complete;
- ✅ requires a previously identified problem to be corrected and verified;
- 🧭 means change strategy while preserving valid work;
- 🛑 means real safe stop/blocker;
- ♻️ means only a bounded part must be redone;
- 💀 is exceptional and only for a genuinely unusable overall implementation/approach;
- visual status never invents progress or changes application behavior.
